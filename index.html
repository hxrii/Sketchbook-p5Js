About this page

This page is my playground for experiments with simulations, particles, algorithms, and interactive sketches. Every project teaches me something new, and I'll keep adding more as I continue exploring the creative side of programming.

My biggest inspiration comes from Daniel Shiffman, The Coding Train, and The Nature of Code. Their passion for teaching has completely changed the way I look at programming—not just as a tool to solve problems, but as a medium for creativity, play, and exploration.

Thanks to the amazing work of Processing.org, p5.js makes it incredibly easy to turn ideas into interactive visuals. It lowers the barrier to creative coding, making programming feel more like sketching on a digital canvas than writing software.
